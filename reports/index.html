Wall Street heads into Nvidia's first earnings report since Jensen Huang's ouster already on the back foot: the S&P 500 fell 1.92% and the Nasdaq 100 dropped 2.67% in Tuesday's session, with chipmakers Micron (-7.62%) and AMD (-5.58%) leading losses after fresh US export curbs on AI chips to China hit sentiment. Options markets imply Nvidia's results could swing its market value by roughly $335 billion, a bet that reflects both the stock's 35% slide from October highs and lingering uncertainty as interim CEO Colette Kress makes her earnings-call debut. Away from chips, Home Depot beat estimates while naming Richard McPhail as CEO, and Disney topped forecasts on parks and streaming strength, offering pockets of resilience. Fed officials sent mixed signals, with St. Louis's Musalem pushing for rate hikes as inflation runs hot even as Kevin Warsh argued the AI investment boom could sustain stronger long-term growth. With the 10-year yield at 5.14% and the VIX at 22.96, markets are bracing for a pivotal 24 hours.

Three things to know

  2. Chip stocks are already down double digits from highs: Micron -7.6%, AMD -5.6%, SanDisk -8.6%
