Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive on Monday, less than two weeks after his ouster amid a personal-conduct investigation, after receiving input from senior US officials who found no national security concern; shares surged roughly 29-30%. The reinstatement, which President Trump had publicly urged, triggered a broad risk-on rally that reversed much of last week's AI-driven rout: the Nasdaq 100 gained 6.1%, the S&P 500 rose 4.3% and the Dow added 2.5%, with breadth strongly positive at 408 advancers against 93 decliners.
